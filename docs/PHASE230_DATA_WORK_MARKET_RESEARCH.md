# Phase 230 — Data Work Market and Compliance Research

Research snapshot: 12 November 2026. Public project listings and eligibility change;
re-check official terms and the live account dashboard before acting.

## Candidate channels

### Outlier
- Official opportunity listings include remote English writing / AI evaluator work and
  currently surfaced a United Kingdom location among eligible work locations.
- Typical tasks described by the listings include ranking model responses, writing or
  revising prompts, checking factuality, and evaluating generated image/video/audio.
- The opportunity pages describe flexible freelance work and PayPal/AirTM payouts.
- Pay varies by location, expertise, project, assessment and project availability.
  A displayed rate is not guaranteed earnings or a promise of continuous work.
- Official entry point: https://app.outlier.ai/opportunities

### DataAnnotation
- Official FAQ describes generalist response evaluation, comparison of AI outputs,
  image-generation review, coding evaluation, multilingual work and specialist STEM /
  professional tracks.
- Its FAQ advertises example starting rates, but these are platform statements, not
  verified offers to this account. Access is qualification- and demand-dependent.
- The starter assessment is described as one attempt; prepare carefully and do not
  rush it. Payment is described as PayPal.
- Official FAQ: https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs

### Upwork
- Public data-annotation listings include computer-vision annotation, audio
  segmentation, AI evaluation, data labeling and QA work.
- The marketplace is suitable for project-based work where a client can define the
  deliverable, sample, acceptance criteria, price and milestones.
- Proposals consume Connects; the freelancer pricing page currently shows Basic at
  $0/month and additional Connects priced separately. Check the actual proposal cost
  before bidding.
- Upwork's current automation policy defines bots broadly and warns that unapproved
  automation, scraping, auto-refresh and browser extensions can cause account
  restrictions. It says API access must be requested and approved for compliant
  automation; even an API key does not authorize every action.
- Yuki must not automate Upwork page browsing, scraping, proposal submission, account
  activity or clicks. Use the platform manually unless a specific approved API scope
  authorizes a connector action.
- Official listings: https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/data-annotation/
- Freelancer pricing: https://www.upwork.com/pricing/freelancer
- Automation policy: https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467-Use-bots-and-other-automation-properly

### Clickworker / UHRS
- Clickworker says registration can be temporarily disabled in countries where demand
  is low or supply is sufficient; available payout methods vary by country.
- Its current UHRS payment explanation describes a 39-day quality-check period before
  earnings become payable. This makes it less suitable when immediate cash flow is
  the priority.
- Check account registration and payment details before investing time.
- Official help: https://support-workplace.clickworker.com/

### Toloka
- Toloka's eligibility page includes geographic and identity/payment restrictions;
  eligibility is also project-specific.
- The Toloker agreement says automated methods such as scripts/robots are prohibited
  unless the specific task terms allow them. A project may also restrict generative AI
  tools or third-party assistance.
- Therefore Yuki must not assist inside Toloka tasks unless the specific task terms
  explicitly permit the assistance being used.
- Official eligibility: https://toloka.ai/legal/eligibility-and-geographic-restrictions
- Official terms: https://toloka.ai/tolokers/user-agreement-january-2025

## Practical prioritization

1. Apply manually to one or two specialist/generalist AI evaluation tracks where the
   worker's real skills fit (Outlier and DataAnnotation are candidate channels).
2. Prepare a concise, truthful skills profile and practice with public, non-client
   examples. Do not use Yuki to answer a live qualification test unless its rules
   explicitly permit AI assistance.
3. Use Upwork selectively for clearly scoped computer-vision, audio or QA projects.
   Calculate net hourly value after Connects, platform fees, unpaid qualification,
   client review/rework and payout timing.
4. Treat Clickworker/UHRS as a secondary option due to variable registration/work
   availability and delayed UHRS payout.
5. Treat Toloka as task-by-task permission-gated; no scripts or AI assistance unless
   the exact task permits it.

## Yuki's permitted role

Yuki can help create a truthful profile, explain public rubrics, train on synthetic
examples, organize a project, prepare permitted local annotation drafts, check
consistency, track review outcomes, calculate effective hourly earnings and prepare
owner-reviewed exports. The worker remains responsible for qualification tests,
identity verification, actual work and truthful representations.

Before processing client assets, Yuki must check the contract, NDA, data-processing
terms, platform rules and client instructions. Do not upload confidential or personal
data to a model provider without explicit authorization and a suitable data-processing
basis. Some tasks require unaided human judgment or prohibit generative AI assistance;
the workbench must support a per-project "AI assistance prohibited" setting before
real client work is enabled.

## Revenue and hardware policy

- Count revenue only when the platform/client confirms and pays it.
- Keep gross, fees, refunds, rejected work, tax reserve and net received separate.
- Do not borrow, buy hardware or promise a purchase based on advertised rates,
  estimated earnings or unaccepted submissions.
- Select hardware only after measuring actual local inference, annotation throughput,
  VRAM/RAM, storage, power and workload requirements.
